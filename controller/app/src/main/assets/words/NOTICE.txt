words-en-gb.txt: a list of common English words (British spelling) ordered by how common they are, used for
predictive text in the Phone Guard launcher.

Made from:
- the order of words in Peter Norvig's count_1w.txt (https://norvig.com/ngrams/), derived from the Google Web
  Trillion Word Corpus. Only the ordering is used, no counts. Norvig's page licenses his code under MIT;
  the licence of the data itself is not stated there, so check before any public release.
- the spelling filter and British forms from SCOWL (http://wordlist.aspell.net), hunspell en_GB-ise 2026.02.25,
  whose licence and copyright are in NOTICE-SCOWL.txt.
- a few texting words and common contractions added by hand.
