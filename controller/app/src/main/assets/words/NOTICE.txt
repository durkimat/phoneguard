words-en-gb.txt: a list of common English words (British spelling), most likely first, used for predictive text in the Phone Guard
launcher. Rebuilt by tools/build_wordlist.py in the repository.

Made from:
- Word order: how often words occur in film and TV dialogue, from the OpenSubtitles frequency list en_50k.txt in
  https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords (generated from the OPUS OpenSubtitles 2018 corpus). That content is licensed
  CC-BY-SA-4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). This word list is derived from it and is therefore shared
  under the same licence, CC-BY-SA-4.0, with credit to the FrequencyWords project and OpenSubtitles. The app's code is
  licensed separately.
- Words the dialogue list lacks come after it, in the order of the web-based ranking of Peter Norvig's count_1w.txt
  (https://norvig.com/ngrams/; derived from the Google Web Trillion Word Corpus). Only the ordering is used, no counts. Norvig's
  page licenses his code under MIT; the licence of that data is not stated there: check before any public release.
- The spelling filter and British forms come from SCOWL (http://wordlist.aspell.net), hunspell en_GB-ise 2026.02.25, whose
  licence and copyright are in NOTICE-SCOWL.txt.
- Common contractions and a few texting words were placed by hand; strong swear words are left out of the suggestions.
