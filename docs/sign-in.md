# Signing in to the parent page

The parent page has two ways in. You can use either one at any time.

## Your parent link (always works)

The setup tool gave you a private link that ends in `#k=` and a long key. Opening it signs that browser in. **Save the whole link in your password manager.** It is the way back in if you lose every device, so keep it private.

## Passkeys (the easy way)

A passkey is the fingerprint, face or screen PIN that your phone or computer already uses. There is nothing to type or paste, and nothing to forward by accident. Each device has its own passkey.

1. Open the parent page with your private link.
2. Press **Set up a passkey** when the page offers it. Or open **Sign-in and passkeys** (on the home page, or under **The phone** tab) and press **Add a passkey on this device**.
3. Use the device's fingerprint, face or screen PIN when it asks.

Next time, open the page's address and press **Sign in with a passkey**. A browser you sign in this way stays signed in for 30 days after you last used it.

## Adding another phone or computer

1. On a device that is signed in, open **Sign-in and passkeys** and press **Add another device**.
2. On the other device, open the link shown (or scan the code). It works once, for 10 minutes.
3. Press **Add this device** and use its fingerprint, face or screen PIN.

## Signing out

- **Sign out of this browser** forgets the sign-in on this browser.
- **Sign out everywhere** ends every browser that signed in with a passkey. The parent link still works, so you cannot lock yourself out.
- **Remove** next to a passkey stops that device signing in. Do this for a lost phone or laptop.

## Good to know

- The device must have a screen lock (PIN, pattern, fingerprint or face). Without one it cannot make a passkey.
- Passkeys belong to the page's address. If you ever move your companion to a new address, add passkeys again; the parent link keeps working.
- Passkeys need this update of the companion (0.7.0 or newer): see [updating](updating.md).
