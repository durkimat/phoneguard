# Install Phone Guard on a Qin J36

Pick your computer's system and follow that guide from the top. Each guide is a single list of numbered steps.

- **[Windows](install/windows.md)**
- **[macOS](install/macos.md)**
- **[Linux](install/linux.md)**

## What you need

- A **Qin J36**, charged to at least half, and a USB-C cable that carries data.
- A **computer** with about 5 GB free disk space. You do not need to install anything else yourself in advance.
- **Nothing on the phone that you want to keep.** The steps erase it.
- A free **Cloudflare account** and your own **smartphone**, for the last steps.
