# Family Chat 1.0.0-rc6: test checklist (for the owner)

rc6 adds a large sticker library and chat themes. Install over rc5 (same key). Earlier checklists still apply.

**Stickers**
1. Tap the smiley: the tray shows a search box and tabs Recent, Animated, Happy, Love, Animals, Food, Sports, Party, Weather, Words. Each tab has stickers; animated ones have a small green dot.
2. Search: type "heart", "dog", "pizza", "party": matching stickers appear; nonsense shows "No sticker matches that search."; clearing the box returns to the tab.
3. Send one: the group sees "Sent by <child>" and then the sticker. Send a second sticker straight away: NO second "Sent by" line. Wait more than 2 minutes, or let anyone post or react in between, then send another: the line is back. In another chat, the line is always sent.
4. Send an animated one: it moves for the group (and on the child's screen).
5. Recent: the tab lists the last stickers sent, newest first, at most 24, still there after closing the app.
6. Look through the library for anything unsuitable for a child (drink, smoking, weapons, pills, rude or suggestive things, skulls, flags) and tell us what you find: none should be there.
7. About and risks (Admin) lists the sticker credits (Microsoft Fluent Emoji, Noto Emoji CC BY 4.0, Unicode CLDR).
8. The sending limits are unchanged: a burst of stickers still says "Sending too fast. Try again in a minute." and never half-sends.

**Chat theme** (picture button at the top right of a chat)
9. The screen is called "Chat theme": a preview, a row "My bubbles" with six colours (Green, Blue, Pink, Lilac, Peach, Grey), then wallpaper tabs Colours, Patterns, Nature, Space, Fun; Colours also has "From my photos". "Use this theme" keeps it for this chat only; another chat is unaffected; no PIN is asked.
10. Only the child's own bubbles change colour; others' stay white.
11. Try the dark wallpapers (Stars, Galaxy, Aurora, Moon, Deep blue, Night) and Forest: names, times and the "edited" text are light and readable, reaction chips stay readable, incoming bubbles stay white with dark text. On Sunset, Desert, Hills, Beach and Berry the names and times sit on a small white chip.
12. A chat that used a wallpaper chosen in rc2 or later (Sand, Mint, Sky, Lilac, Dots, Stripes, Confetti, Night, From my photos) still has it.
13. Check the app size (about 41 MB) and that nothing slows down when opening the tray.
