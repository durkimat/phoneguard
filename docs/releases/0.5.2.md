Phone Guard app 0.5.2, companion 0.8.13.

Radios now go off 1 minute after the phone is locked, and the parent page has a radio log to check it.
Also update the companion (setup tool > Update my companion) for the new setting and the log.

**Radios while locked**
- Their own delay, per mode: 30 seconds, 1 (the default), 2, 5 or 10 minutes. Before, they shared the 5 minute "Screen off after".
- That setting is now called "Close apps once the screen has been off", which is what else it does. Its value is unchanged.
- Battery and power saver shows how long the phone was locked with every radio off, what kept a radio on, a warning if one was on when it should not have been, and the recent changes. It arrives about one check-in late.
- Fixed: after music, a download or start-up, the radios could stay on until the next check-in.
- Fixed: a radio that comes back on while the phone is locked is switched off again.
- "Power saver: only report" also keeps the radios on; the page now says so.

**Also**
- The parent page says plainly whether a newer companion or app is available.

**Updating**
Update from the parent page (about 48 MB, on Wi-Fi). Your phones, settings and parent link are kept.
