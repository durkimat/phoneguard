Phone Guard app 0.5.3. The companion does not change (0.8.13).

The Classic Mac look with striped windows now carries its title stripes along the top bar, wrapping round each symbol.

**Mac look, Windows set to Stripes**
- The stripes run between the symbols and follow their shapes, in the same colour as the window title's stripes.
- The home screen no longer has a separate "Home" title bar. Weather and clock move up to where the other looks have them.
- Torch, alarm and timer have fixed places, the torch is smaller, and the clock no longer crowds the timer.
- A radio that is on but not in use is darker than before; a switched-off one has a solid slash.

Nokia looks and Mac windows set to Platinum or Flat look as they did.

**Updating**
Update from the parent page (about 48 MB, on Wi-Fi). Your phones, settings and parent link are kept. No companion update is needed.
