Phone Guard app 0.5.4. The companion does not change (0.8.13).

A data plan saved on the phone now takes priority, and the top bar and home screen are tidier.

**Mobile data**
- A plan saved in Phone Guard drives the home screen, the Mobile data page and what the parent page shows. Android's plan is used only when none is saved.
- After updating, check Allowance, Renews, any Correction, and "Set by: this phone" on the Mobile data page.
- The page keeps the allowance exactly as typed and refreshes after saving.

**Top bar and home**
- Alarm and timer show under the home clock only, not in the top bar. The date sits a little lower.
- The torch matches the size of the other symbols.
- The Nokia looks have a bigger top bar clock.
- Highlight boxes in the top bar are centred, and now show in the Mac looks.

**Updating**
Update from the parent page (about 48 MB, on Wi-Fi). Your phones, settings and parent link are kept. No companion update is needed.
