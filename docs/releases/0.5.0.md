Phone Guard app 0.5.0, companion 0.8.11 (unchanged).

New: a picture Gallery (Extras), WhatsApp photos, stickers, GIFs and videos, an emoji and sticker picker, and reactions.
Changed: Back now goes back the way you came.
This is a big update and not yet tried on a real phone or live WhatsApp: update when you have the phone with you.

**Pictures and messages**
- Gallery: everything on the phone in a grid, opening on the camera photos, newest by date taken.
- WhatsApp pictures show on the message; OK opens them. They download by themselves only on Wi-Fi; otherwise press OK to fetch one. Videos need Wi-Fi.
- Deleted-for-everyone and edited messages now update on the phone.
- On the writing screen, press Down for emoji and (WhatsApp) stickers.

**Changes to how keys behave**
- Back retraces your steps, including through option menus, the dialler and game help. After unlocking, a call or another app you stay where you were (Home if the mode has changed).
- Spotify Playlists and radio presets: the right key is Back; the left key is Options.

**Under the hood**
- The shared WhatsApp code and bridge were substantially rebuilt. After updating, check that messages send and arrive.
- The WhatsApp message store is upgraded. The phone cannot be put back to 0.4.83; any fix will be a newer version.

**Updating**
Update from the parent page (about 48 MB, on Wi-Fi). Your phones, settings and parent link are kept. Afterwards press Home and check you see Phone Guard's own screens.
