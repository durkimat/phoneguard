Phone Guard app 0.5.1, companion 0.8.12.

New: About phone (Settings > This phone) shows the version and how an update is going, with Check now.
Fixed: the emoji and sticker picker, and a writing screen that could be left with no Send.
Also update the companion (setup tool > Update my companion) so the parent page can say what an update is waiting for.

**Seeing updates**
- About phone shows the last check-in and the update: waiting (and why), downloading, installing, failed (and why) or done. Check now checks in at once.
- A short notice on the phone when an update starts, fails or has happened.
- The phone says "done" only once the new version is installed, and reports a failure as soon as it can.
- This update itself is carried out by 0.5.0, which shows no progress: check the version. About phone and the notice appear once 0.5.1 is running.

**Fixes**
- Emoji and stickers are one scrolling grid: recent ones first, then every group. Moving down reaches everything.
- Leaving a picker without choosing now returns you to your message, with Send. On the buttons under the message, the right key is Back.

**Updating**
Update from the parent page (about 48 MB, on Wi-Fi). Your phones, settings and parent link are kept.
