(https://github.com/tulir/whatsmeow), version go.mau.fi/whatsmeow v0.0.0-20261107160454-79dc2a8c33df,
licensed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0 (https://www.mozilla.org/MPL/2.0/). The library is used unmodified;
its source is available at https://github.com/tulir/whatsmeow/tree/79dc2a8c33df.
Family Chat also uses AndroidX Media3 Transformer and Effect 1.4.1 (Apache-2.0) to prepare videos for sending.
Other components: the Go standard library and golang.org/x modules (BSD-3-Clause), protobuf (BSD-3-Clause),
gomobile (BSD-3-Clause), and the Android SDK libraries (Apache-2.0).
(Before a release, regenerate this list from wabridge/go.sum.)

Risk: using an unofficial client is against WhatsApp's terms of service. WhatsApp can block or ban the account
it is linked to, without notice. Use an account you can afford to lose; the apps limit messages per minute and per day
to lower, not remove, that risk. Messages are held on the phone only, and only for groups the parent has allowed.
