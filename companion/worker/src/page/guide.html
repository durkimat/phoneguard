How Phone Guard works

Your family has a small private service in your own Cloudflare account. It holds the settings and passes them to the child's phone. Nobody else runs it or can see it.

Who does what

Your computerRuns the setup tool once. It creates the service in your Cloudflare account and makes the codes.
Your Cloudflare accountHolds the settings. The free plan is enough.
Child's phoneChecks in about every 15 minutes and follows the timetable. If it loses internet it keeps the last settings.
Your own phoneOpens this page to approve phones, change the timetable and connect Spotify.

Adding a phone

  1. On this page press Add a phone. You get a QR code, an address and a code. They work for about 10 minutes.
  2. On the child's phone open Phone Guard, press Connect to the family companion, scan the QR code or type the address and code.
  3. The child's phone shows 4 digits. Type them here under New phone waiting and press Approve. This proves you are approving the phone in your hand.
  4. Write down the recovery key shown. It appears once and lets you take back control if you lose your private link.
  5. Within about 10 seconds the child's phone says it is paired.

Changing the timetable

Press Timetable and settings on a phone. Add a block for each part of the day, choose its mode, press Save. The phone picks it up at its next check-in, or sooner if you press Sync now on the phone. Times follow the time zone shown on the page, including clock changes.

What you can control

Open a phone from your page to find these (each is a section you can open):

  • Right now: force a mode, give a temporary boost (up to 3 hours), set holiday dates.
  • Timetable: the weekly blocks, base mode and time zone.
  • Apps and modes: a tick grid of which apps each mode allows, approving new apps, asking the phone to remove an app.
  • Mode settings: screen, sound, calls, camera, location, notifications, radio, WhatsApp checks and more, per mode.
  • Phone restrictions, WhatsApp, Radio stations, Apps on the Nokia menu, Weather place, Look of the phone.
  • Spotify, Screen time, Battery and power saver, Updates and removals.
  • Manage this phone: rename, copy settings from another phone, release or forget it.

Changes reach the phone at its next check-in (about 15 minutes), or straight away if you press Sync now on the phone.

Spotify

  1. Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard with a Premium account and create an app.
  2. Use the redirect address shown in the Spotify box on the phone's settings page, and tick Web API.
  3. Under Users and Access add the email of each Spotify account that will be used.
  4. Copy the Client ID into the Spotify box and press Connect Spotify. Sign in to Spotify when asked.

The sign-in key is locked in your browser so that only the child's phone can open it. Your Cloudflare service only ever holds the locked version.

WhatsApp

This lets the child's phone read and send WhatsApp messages through its own simple screens, with the people you choose. Messages stay on the phone. The phone becomes a "linked device" of one WhatsApp account, the same way WhatsApp Web does. You need the smartphone that has that WhatsApp account (the child's own number if they have one, otherwise yours).

  1. On the child's phone open the pairing screen (Messages, then WhatsApp, or Settings, then WhatsApp; Messages only offers WhatsApp when the current mode allows WhatsApp checks). It is titled Link WhatsApp and asks for Your WhatsApp number: type it with the country code and no plus (for example 447700900123) and press Get code. The phone shows a code.
  2. On the smartphone that has that WhatsApp account open WhatsApp, then Settings (or the three dots), Linked devices, Link a device, then Link with phone number instead, and type the code from the child's phone.
  3. The child's phone says Linked. If the code runs out, press New code and try again.
  4. Choose who the child may message: open the phone on your parent page, then WhatsApp. Nobody is allowed until you add numbers (one per line, country code, no plus, or g: and a group name) or tick everyone.

Open WhatsApp on this phone (WhatsApp cannot be opened straight onto its Linked devices screen, so follow the taps above).

WhatsApp can lapse a linked device if your main smartphone has not been online for about two weeks. If messages stop, link again from step 1.

Good to know

  • Your private link opens this page already signed in. Anyone with it can manage the phones, so keep it private.
  • To pair a phone again from scratch, use Disconnect from the family companion on its enrol screen.
