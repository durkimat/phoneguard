How Phone Guard works

Your family has a small private service in your own Cloudflare account. It holds the settings and passes them to the child's phone. Nobody else runs it or can see it.

Who does what

Your computerRuns the setup tool once. It creates the service in your Cloudflare account and makes the codes.
Your Cloudflare accountHolds the settings. The free plan is enough.
Child's phoneChecks in about every 15 minutes and follows the timetable. If it loses internet it keeps the last settings.
Your own phoneOpens this page to approve phones, change the timetable and connect Spotify.

Adding a phone

  1. On this page press Add a phone. You get a QR code, an address and a code. They work for about 10 minutes.
  2. On the child's phone open Phone Guard, press Connect to the family companion, scan the QR code or type the address and code.
  3. The child's phone shows 4 digits. Type them here under New phone waiting and press Approve. This proves you are approving the phone in your hand.
  4. Write down the recovery key shown. It appears once and lets you take back control if you lose your private link.
  5. Within about 10 seconds the child's phone says it is paired.

Changing the timetable

Press Timetable and settings on a phone. Add a block for each part of the day, choose its mode, press Save. The phone picks it up at its next check-in, or sooner if you press Sync now on the phone. Times follow the time zone shown on the page, including clock changes.

Spotify

  1. Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard with a Premium account and create an app.
  2. Use the redirect address shown in the Spotify box on the phone's settings page, and tick Web API.
  3. Under Users and Access add the email of each Spotify account that will be used.
  4. Copy the Client ID into the Spotify box and press Connect Spotify. Sign in to Spotify when asked.

The sign-in key is locked in your browser so that only the child's phone can open it. Your Cloudflare service only ever holds the locked version.

Good to know

  • Your private link opens this page already signed in. Anyone with it can manage the phones, so keep it private.
  • To pair a phone again from scratch, use Disconnect from the family companion on its enrol screen.
